# Third-party materials

The DESPOTIC runtime and derived GOW chemistry modules retain their source
copyright notices and GNU GPL version 3 or later terms. LAMDA molecular data
and Visser shielding tables retain their original scientific attribution.
These dependencies are separate from the RoSE simulation, which is not
distributed here. No upstream copyright notices are removed, and no new
repository-wide relicensing of third-party code is asserted.

Historical absolute paths in frozen configurations/manifests are provenance.
Use the portable replay commands for relocation. Original job scripts are
provided as scientific provenance, not as automatically runnable submissions.
